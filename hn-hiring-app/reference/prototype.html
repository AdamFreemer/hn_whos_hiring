Method. Threads are every story by the whoishiring account whose title contains “who is hiring” (excluding the sibling “Who wants to be hired?” and “Freelancer?” threads). Counts are post-level: a technology scores once per top-level job post that mentions it, not once per raw occurrence, mirroring hntrends.com and keeping one verbose listing from skewing totals. Matching is case-insensitive on word boundaries. Labels marked ≈ (Go, Spring, Express, Phoenix, C) collide with ordinary English words and are best read as upper bounds. JavaScript counts the literal word “javascript,” not “JS,” to avoid double-counting Node.js / Next.js. Source: the free public Hacker News Search API (Algolia). Nothing is stored.
